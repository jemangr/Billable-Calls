# Privacy, Billable Calls

- **On the phone only.** The app reads the phone's call log and contacts to log calls with your customers. The calls, the companies, the notes and the settings are kept in the app's private storage on the phone.
- **No server, no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.**
- **Google Drive (optional).** If you connect it, the app uses Google's «drive.file» permission. That means it can see only the files it creates itself: the «Billable Calls» folder, the «Πελάτες» sheet, the exports and the backups. It sees nothing else in your Drive. The app keeps no password or key; Google gives it a short-lived token each time.
- **Customer list from a link (optional).** The app downloads the file from the link you enter.
- **Updates (optional, can be turned off).** The app asks GitHub's public API whether a newer version exists. Nothing about you is sent.
- **Bug report.** It is sent only by you: directly (over HTTPS through platebit.app, which keeps nothing) or from your email app, to support@jemangr.app. It contains the version, the phone model, settings and counts. It never contains numbers, names, companies or notes. Privacy questions: privacy@jemangr.app.
- **Deleting.** Uninstalling the app deletes everything on the phone. Backups on your Drive stay there until you delete them.
