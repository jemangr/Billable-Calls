## Billable Calls 1.0

The first version.

- **Calls and companies:** every call is matched to a company, from the contact's «Company» field or from the contact name (company before or after the person's name, any separator). Matching is automatic only when it is sure.
- **Lists:** «Business» and «Other» tabs, with the contact name first. Long-press to select, then remove calls from business or add them to a company: only these calls, or every call of the number from now on.
- **After-call card:** asks if the call was business, which company (with search, or a new one), the topic, billable yes/no and a note.
- **Billable time:** minimum length (1′, 5′, 10′, 15′ or your own) and rounding up, per company if you like.
- **Reminders:** for missed calls and unanswered outgoing calls, on your own hours (Mon–Fri, Saturday and Sunday apart, quiet hours). They stop when you talk, after N reminders, after a set time or with «Ignore», whichever comes first.
- **Two SIMs:** choose which one counts.
- **Export:** Excel, CSV or PDF, for one company or all of them, shared or saved straight to Google Drive.
- **Customer list:** from a CSV/Excel file, a link (Google Sheets too) or a sheet on Google Drive.
- **Backup:** daily to Google Drive, or to a file.
- **Other:** Greek and English, automatic updates, bug report by email.

**Install:** download `BillableCalls.apk` below and open it on the phone. Android asks once to allow installing from your browser or file manager.
